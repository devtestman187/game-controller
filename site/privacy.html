Privacy policy

For the Thumb Party app (phones and tablets) and the Thumb Party TV app (Android TV and Windows). Effective October 29, 2026.

In short: the apps don't collect, sell or share any personal information. They have no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or advertising tools. Everything they need stays on your devices and your home Wi-Fi.

What the apps do with data

  • Controller input stays on your Wi-Fi. The phone sends button presses, stick positions and tilt directly to the TV app on the same local network. Nothing goes over the internet.
  • Pairing and settings stay on the device. To reconnect without the code, the phone and the TV app each save a random pairing code and a random device ID. Your settings (vibration strength, layout, and so on) are saved on the device too. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Device names. The TV app shows each phone as something like "iPhone 3F1C" and the phone shows the TV's name, only on the local network.
  • Diagnostics. The phone's Diagnostics screen shows connection details, battery and temperature readings. They aren't sent anywhere. If you tap Share, the report goes only to the app and person you choose.

Permissions

  • Local network: to find and connect to the TV app on your Wi-Fi.
  • Vibration: for the buzz when you press a button or something happens in the game.
  • Motion sensors: for gyro mode, where tilting the phone steers. The readings are turned into controller input on the phone and sent only to the TV app.

Children

Thumb Party is made for families to play together, and children may play with an adult. Because the apps collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children.

Future changes

A later test version may add anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, how often each control layout is used, and connection speed), to make the controls better. If it does, this policy will be updated first, the TV app will ask an adult before anything is shared, and the statistics will never include names, contact details or advertising IDs. Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
