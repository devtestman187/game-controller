# Milestone 5 checklist: TV-to-phone haptics and the test game

Copied from the PRD acceptance tests. Run it with the `m5` builds: the iPhone `.ipa` from Codemagic (tag `m5`), the TV APK (0.5.0) and, if you like, the Windows TV app (0.5.0). Android phone items are marked *(deferred)*.

Setup: install the TV APK and open **Controller Test** on the TV. Its main screen should say **4 of 4 event sounds loaded**. Open **Controller** on the iPhone (it reconnects). Two new buttons sit at the top right of the TV screen (press **Up** on the remote to reach them):
- **Haptic test**: four big buttons, **Hit**, **Success**, **Error** and **Rumble**, sending that haptic event to the phone and playing its sound on the TV. **Intensity** and **Duration** are set with − and +.
- **Test game**: move the blue sprite to the green goal past the grey walls and the red hazards. D-pad, tilt (gyro) or the left stick (sticks) moves; **A** dashes; the right stick moves an aim cursor. **OK** on the remote shows or hides the debug overlay; **Back** leaves.

What the four events should feel like on the phone (first proposal, tell me what to change):

| Event | Feel | Sound | In the game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hit | One heavy thump that fades | Crunch | Bumping a wall or the edge, or a hazard touching you |
| Success | Three rising taps, "da-da-DAA" | Computer noise (5 s long) | Reaching the goal |
| Error | Two short, sharp buzzes | Slime (4.7 s long) | Pressing A while the dash is cooling down (the ring around the sprite) |
| Rumble | A low, wobbling buzz | Force field | Dashing |

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] **Each of the four events from the panel feels different (iPhone).** TV → **Haptic test** → press each button a few times. *(Expect: four distinct feels as in the table, each with its sound on the TV. Try **Intensity** 30% and 100%, and **Duration** 100 ms and 2 s on Rumble.)*
- [ ] *(deferred)* The same on the Android phone.
- [ ] **The game is playable end to end with the D-pad, the gyro and the sticks.** Play at least one round in each mode (switch modes on the phone mid-game). *(Expect: reaching the goal shows "Goal!", and a mirrored round starts after about 2 s. Tilt and the left stick move slower when pushed less; the D-pad always moves at full speed.)*
- [ ] **Each game event fires its haptic and its sound together.** Bump a wall, let a hazard touch you, reach the goal, dash, and press A again straight after a dash. *(Expect: hit, hit, success, rumble, error, each felt on the phone with the matching sound on the TV. Sliding along a wall doesn't keep buzzing.)*
- [ ] **All four sounds play on both TV builds.** On the TV, and on the Windows app if you use it: the main screen says **4 of 4 event sounds loaded**, and each panel button is audible. *(Both builds already passed an automatic check, see below; this is to hear them.)* The files are listed in `assets/CREDITS.md`.
- [ ] **Repeated events and backgrounding keep haptics working.** Press Hit 20+ times quickly. Then swipe the Controller app home for a few seconds, come back and press each event again. *(Expect: every press is felt, and after coming back they still work (the iOS haptic engine restarts). Phone Diagnostics → Haptics → "TV events" counts them.)*
- [ ] **With the haptics switch off, TV events play nothing.** Phone → vibration icon → turn **Haptics** off → press the panel buttons. *(Expect: no vibration, while the sounds still play on the TV. Turn it back on afterwards.)*
- [ ] **Replacing a sound file needs no code change.** On the PC: copy any `.ogg` over `assets/audio/hit.ogg` (or put a `hit.wav` there instead and delete the `.ogg`), rebuild the TV app, press Hit. *(Expect: the new sound. `git checkout assets/audio` restores the original.)*

## Extra checks (not in the PRD, quick)
- [ ] The remote works everywhere: **Up** from the main screen reaches **Test game** and **Haptic test**, the panel's buttons and − / + work with arrows and OK, and **Back** leaves both screens.
- [ ] The debug overlay (**OK** in the game) shows the phone, Connected, the ping, the mode (D-pad, Gyro or Sticks), the live values and the last haptic event sent.
- [ ] Resizing the Windows game window keeps the sprite in the same place on the field and the speed feels the same.
- [ ] The TV log lists each event sent, e.g. `[haptic] hit 70% 120 ms (game: wall) → 1 controller`.
- [ ] The intensity slider on the phone also scales the TV's events (it's one setting for ticks and events for now).

## Already verified on the PC (2026-10-25)
- Automated: 44 protocol tests (haptic message, ranges, unknown event names ignored), 55 networking tests (events reach a paired phone in order, sent to every paired phone but not unpaired ones, a phone sending one is closed, an unknown event from a newer TV keeps the connection), 107 phone tests (patterns: distinct shapes, intensity and duration scaling, rumble length; widget tests: each event plays in D-pad, gyro and stick mode, switch off plays nothing, skipped in the background and working again after, a native error doesn't stop the pad, Diagnostics counts), 18 game tests (speeds the same in any window, D-pad full speed and analog scaled, wall bump fires once, light pushes don't buzz, dash rumble and cooldown error, no tunneling through walls, hazards knock back without ending the round, goal and mirrored next round, resizing), 17 TV tests (panel sends each event to the phone with its sound, steppers, remote, no controller; game sends to the driving controller with sounds, debug overlay keys, Esc, small window; remote reaches every button).
- **Sounds on both TV builds**: an on-device test (`tv_test_app/integration_test/sound_test.dart`) loaded all four OGG files through flutter_soloud (hit 1.55 s, success 5.00 s, error 4.71 s, rumble 0.95 s) and played each one (playback advanced about 0.5 s in 0.5 s), on the **Windows** build and on the **Android TV** itself. No WAV conversion needed.
- Builds: phone APK, TV APK (flutter_soloud compiled for Android with the NDK) and Windows release. **TV APK 0.5.0 is installed on the TV.**
- The game, the panel and the main screen rendered on Windows with real fonts at 960 × 540 and 600 × 800.
