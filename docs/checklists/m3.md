# Milestone 3 checklist: Gyro mode

Copied from the PRD acceptance tests. Run it with the `m3` builds: the iPhone `.ipa` from Codemagic (tag `m3`) and the TV APK. Android phone items are marked *(deferred)*.

Setup: open **Controller Test** on the TV and **Controller** on the iPhone (it reconnects to the TV). At the top of the phone, tap **Gyro**. The screen locks to the way you're holding the phone, the D-pad is replaced by a circle with a dot, and the first reading becomes the center. Hold the phone the way you want to play and tap **Calibrate** whenever you want a new center. The tune icon (next to the mode switch) opens sensitivity, dead zone and smoothing. On the TV, the controller's picture shows a stick with a dot and "Tilt x … y …".

## Acceptance tests
- [X] **Neutral reads zero.** Hold the phone still at your playing angle and tap **Calibrate**. *(Expect: the TV shows "Tilt x 0.00 y 0.00" (or within ±0.05) and the dot in the middle.)*
- [X] **Directions (iPhone).** Tilt right, left, forward (top edge away from you) and back. *(Expect: the dot and x/y follow smoothly: right = x up to +1, left = x to −1, forward = y to −1 (up), back = y to +1 (down). This also works lying flat (tip the edges down) and upright (turn it like a steering wheel for left/right). **If any direction is reversed, note which**, since the axes can only be confirmed on a real phone.)*
- [ ] *(deferred)* The same on the Android phone.
- [X] **Dead zone and sensitivity.** Tune icon → raise **Dead zone** to 20–30%: small shakes around the center stay at 0.00. Raise **Sensitivity** to 2×: the same tilt gives a bigger value (full deflection at 15° instead of 30°). *(The settings are kept after relaunching.)*
- [X] **Switch modes mid-session.** Switch D-pad → Gyro → D-pad a few times while connected. *(Expect: no reconnect (the TV keeps showing Connected, no "Reconnecting…" on the phone); the TV picture switches between the D-pad and the stick.)*
- [X] **Buttons and ticks in gyro mode.** While tilting, press A, B, X, Y, Start, Select. *(Expect: they light on the TV and tick on the phone; tilting alone never ticks.)*
- [X] **Streaming holds up.** Keep tilting for a minute or two. *(Expect: no visible lag on the TV, no dropped connection. Note the ping line at the top of the phone (the 30 s min/avg/max): the steady stream may keep the phone's Wi-Fi awake and lower the spikes seen in M1.)*

## Extra checks (not in the PRD, quick)
- [ ] Swipe home or pull down Control Center while tilted: the TV shows the stick centered. Coming back resumes with the same calibration.
- [ ] Diagnostics → **Gyro** shows Gyroscope: Working, the landscape side, the live stick values and the update count. Share includes a Gyro line.
- [ ] Smoothing at 0% feels twitchy and at 90% floaty, with 50% in between.
- [ ] TV's **Simulate controller** (and `sim_controller --demo`) now ends each loop by sweeping the stick round a circle.

## Already verified on the PC (2026-10-24)
- Automated: 35 protocol tests (tilt fields), 49 networking tests (tilt reaches the host with the buttons), 63 phone tests (filter follows the gyro and removes drift, ignores shaking, landscape axes, calibration, sensitivity, dead zone, smoothing; widget tests for switching modes without reconnecting, buttons and ticks in gyro mode, no ticks from tilt alone, no gyroscope → stays on the D-pad with a message, leaving the app centers and stops the sensors, settings saved), 6 TV tests.
- The Windows TV app with `sim_controller --demo`: the TV showed the D-pad states, then the stick dot following the tilt sweep (for example "Tilt x 0.69 y 0.40" with the dot lower right) with A held, then back to the D-pad.

## Fixes during testing
- 0.3.1 (tag `m3.1`): switching between D-pad and Gyro turned the iPhone screen 180°. The lock now uses the right landscape side on iOS, and the side is tracked even while lying flat. Re-check: switching modes should no longer rotate the screen, and the tilt directions should still be correct.

- 0.3.2 (tag `m3.2`): starting gyro mode (especially lying flat) could drift off-center, and the 180° turn still happened rarely. Both came from the tilt filter reusing its estimate from the previous gyro session. It now starts fresh and settles before calibrating, and the lock side comes only from the steady side tracker. Re-check: start gyro mode flat on a table several times (the dot stays centered), and switch modes 30+ times (no turn).

## Result
Passed on the iPhone and the TV with 0.3.2 (`m3.2`, 2026-10-24, Android phone deferred). Two issues found during testing were fixed (see above): the 180° turn on mode switches, and drift when starting gyro mode. User: "m3.2 fixed both issues, M3 passes". No tilt direction was reported reversed.
