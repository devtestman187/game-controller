# Milestone 6 checklist: Polish, settings, latency tooling and several controllers

Copied from the PRD acceptance tests, with your answers applied: per-event haptic strength in Settings, and one sprite per controller in the game. Run it with the `m6` builds: the iPhone `.ipa` from Codemagic (tag `m6`), the TV APK (0.6.0) and, if you like, the Windows TV app (0.6.0). Android phone items are marked *(deferred)*.

Setup: install the TV APK and the new iPhone build, open **Controller Test** on the TV and **Controller** on the iPhone. The TV app forgets the old in-memory pairing when it's updated, so enter the code once. From then on the TV remembers the phone.

What's new where:
- **Phone**: a gear icon at the top opens **Settings**: the haptics switch, the **button tick** strength, a strength for **each TV event** (Hit, Success, Error, Rumble) with **Try** buttons, and the gyro and stick settings. The app starts in the mode you used last. The top bar shows your player number (**P1**). **Diagnostics** has two new sections: **Reconnects** and **Session (battery and CPU)**.
- **TV**: a **Latency** button at the top, **Forget phones** and **Save log** next to the other buttons, a player badge and "input ~N ms" on each controller.
- **The four vibrations** were reworked to differ in rhythm and length, not just strength: **hit** one short deep thud, **success** three rising taps and a ring, **error** two long harsh buzzes, **rumble** a strong/weak throb.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] **Measured input latency on home Wi-Fi meets the ~50 ms target, or the gap is documented.** Play for a minute in each mode, then TV → **Latency**. *(Expect: "Input delay, 30 s avg" with a "±" and "Within the 50 ms target". Write down the average, p95 and max for D-pad, Gyro and Sticks, and the ping. If it's above 50 ms, say so and I'll document it with a plan.)*
- [ ] **Saved settings survive closing and reopening the controller app.** Settings → change the tick, two event strengths, a gyro and a stick setting, switch to **Sticks**, then swipe the app away completely and open it again. *(Expect: it opens in Sticks mode, and every value in Settings is as you left it. **Defaults** puts them back.)*
- [ ] **Each TV event's strength can be set on its own, changes how that event feels from the TV, and doesn't change the button tick.** Settings → **Try** each event, then set e.g. Hit to 30% and Rumble to 0% (Off). TV → **Haptic test** → press each button. *(Expect: Hit is clearly weaker, Rumble plays nothing, Success and Error unchanged, and button presses tick as before.)*
- [ ] **The four events are easy to tell apart now.** Eyes closed, have someone press the four panel buttons in random order. *(Tell me which ones you mixed up, if any.)*
- [ ] **A play session of about 30 minutes completes with no dropped connection or stuck input.** Play the game (any modes, gyro for at least 10 minutes) for 30 minutes on battery. Then phone → **Diagnostics**. *(Expect: **Reconnects** says "No drops", nothing stays held on the TV. Note the **Session** line: CPU %, battery drain per hour and heat. The phone shouldn't get hot.)*
- [ ] **The controller recovers after the TV app restarts and after the phone sleeps and wakes.**
  - TV: Back out of Controller Test and open it again (or reinstall it). *(Expect: the phone reconnects within a few seconds without asking for the code.)*
  - Phone: lock it for 30 s, unlock. *(Expect: Connected again within about 2 s of unlocking.)*
  - Also: switch the phone's Wi-Fi off and on. *(Expect: it comes back on its own.)*
  - Phone → **Diagnostics → Reconnects** lists each drop and how long it took to come back.
- [ ] **Two controllers connect at the same time, and the TV shows each one's input separately.** iPhone plus a second controller: the Android phone *(deferred)*, or the TV's **Simulate controller** button, or `sim_controller` on the PC (see docs/building.md). *(Expect: both listed with their own badge (P1, P2), input and ping. In **Test game** each drives its own sprite in its colour, only the phone whose sprite hits a wall feels it, and "P2 wins!" (or P1) when someone reaches the goal.)*
- [ ] **Every milestone's checklist passes on a fresh install from the latest tagged builds.** Delete Controller from the iPhone and Controller Test from the TV, install the `m6` builds, and run the quick pass below.

### Quick pass over the earlier milestones (fresh install)
- [ ] M0: both apps launch; the TV app is in the launcher and works with the remote.
- [ ] M1: the phone finds the TV, the code pairs it, a wrong code is refused, manual IP works, the TV shows the ping, Wi-Fi off/on reconnects. The local network prompt appears on the iPhone.
- [ ] M2: every D-pad direction and button shows on the TV and ticks; a direction plus a button together; turning haptics off stops the ticks.
- [ ] M3: gyro mode calibrates near zero, tilting moves the values the right way, dead zone and sensitivity work, switching modes doesn't disconnect.
- [ ] M4: both sticks and a shoulder button at once, sticks return to center, nothing stays held after a mode switch.
- [ ] M5: the four panel events with their sounds, the game in all three modes, haptics off silences TV events.
