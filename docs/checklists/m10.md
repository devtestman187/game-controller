# Milestone 10 checklist: Puzzle game for 1 player, with game-defined controls

Copied from the PRD v2 acceptance tests (M10). Both apps are **0.10.0**. Android phone and tablet checks stay **pending** (Android testing is on hold); the Android TV and Windows checks don't.

Test with the iPhone (TestFlight) and the Windows TV app (the zip on the GitHub release), or the Android TV (Play). No store forms change: the puzzle only adds more counts to the same anonymous session summary.

## What's new
- **TV: Play** on the home screen opens the game picker: **Shape Race** (new) and the **Arena**.
- **Shape Race**, 1 player: carry each piece from the tray, turn it with **X** and **Y** (or push the right stick left or right in stick mode), and drop it on its outline with **A**. 3 Easy puzzles (House, Rocket, Tree and sun; two pieces to turn in each since m10.1) and 1 Medium (Boat, three to turn), 2 minutes each. A piece close enough to drop makes its outline light up and the phone tick; a right drop snaps and locks ("ka-CHUNK"); a wrong one turns red ("uh-oh"). At the end: the time, or the pieces placed, with **Play again**.
- **TV: Puzzle settings**: every tuning value (tolerances, speeds, delays, time limit), saved, with **Reset to defaults**. Changes count straight away.
- **Phone: the game's controls.** In the puzzle, A says "Pick up", X and Y say "Rotate", and the buttons the puzzle doesn't use are hidden. A small panel in the top bar shows the piece you're holding, turning as it turns, and the time left (red near the end). Back in the Arena, the usual buttons return.
- **Phone: new haptics** for snap, wrong, drop-ready, tick and help (help is for M11), each with its own strength in Settings.
- **Windows: keyboard player** works in the puzzle too: arrows, Space (pick up and drop), X and Z (rotate).
- Under the hood: the `layout` and `status` messages (docs/protocol.md §8a, §8b), games draw from snapshots into any box (S4, S8) and play sounds through the host (S5).

## Acceptance tests
- [X] **An Easy puzzle can be finished with the D-pad, the gyro and the sticks.** Play → Shape Race → House (or any Easy), once in each mode (switch mode on the phone between games). *(Expect: "Done!" with a time each time.)*
- [X] **A piece dropped within tolerance snaps exactly, locks and buzzes. A piece dropped in the wrong spot stays, turns red and buzzes differently. Both feel distinct on the phone.** *(Expect: a crisp click and a firm lock for the snap; a softer, falling "uh-oh" for the wrong drop, with the piece red and a ✗ on it. Pick a red piece up again and it turns back.)*
- [X] **Changing the angle tolerance in playtest settings changes what counts as correct straight away.** Play → Puzzle settings → Angle tolerance down to ±5° (the lowest), then play: a piece dropped a little crooked now turns red. Then up to ±30°: it snaps. Reset to defaults at the end.
- [X] **Holding X and Y rotates smoothly in each direction, and in stick mode the right stick turns the piece.** *(Expect: X turns clockwise ⟳, Y counter-clockwise ⟲, at an even speed while held. Right stick (changed in m10.1): push right to turn clockwise, left for counter-clockwise; a small push turns slowly, a full push faster than X and Y; up and down do nothing.)* *(m10, 2026-10-31: pointing the stick to set the angle was very hard to control. Changed in m10.1.)*
- [X] **A square piece matches its outline at any quarter turn.** *(Automated: `rules_test.dart` drops a square at 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°. None of the four puzzles has a square, so on the phone:)* turn House's wall or door half a turn (upside down) and drop it. *(Expect: it snaps, since a rectangle matches at a half turn; the sun matches at any angle.)*
- [X] **Time-up shows the pieces placed, and a finished puzzle shows the time.** Let one run out. *(Expect: "Time's up!" with "2 of 4 pieces placed" or similar; **Play again** has the remote's focus, so OK starts another.)*
- [ ] *(Added in m10.2)* **The phone works the results.** When a puzzle ends the phone shows A "OK" and B "Back". *(Expect: D-pad or left stick left/right moves between **Play again** and **Back**, A presses the highlighted one, B goes back. The A press that placed the last piece doesn't trigger anything. After Play again the phone shows "Pick up" and "Rotate" again.)* *(m10.2, 2026-11-01: in stick mode, moving the stick lost the focus and A then did nothing; B worked. Fixed for m10.3.)*
- [X] **The arena game is still available from the game picker.** Play → Arena: plays as before.
- [X] **In the puzzle the phone shows "Pick up" on A and "Rotate" on X and Y, hides buttons the puzzle doesn't use, and shows the held piece turning as it rotates. Back on the arena game, the phone returns to its usual buttons.** Check in D-pad, Gyro and Sticks. *(Expect: B is a faint outline, Start and Select gone; in stick mode LB and RB gone too. The panel shows the time left with a bar, and the piece you hold.)*
- [X] **With labels and the status panel showing, no control is smaller than before in any mode.** Compare with the Arena in each mode. *(Also checked by a test in each mode.)*
- [X] **A widget test draws the puzzle at 1280 by 720 in a box smaller than the window and at TV size, and the layout is the same apart from scale. The puzzle's sounds still play, through the host interface.** *(Automated: `packages/puzzle_game/test/view_test.dart`.)*
- [X] **A unit test encodes a puzzle snapshot, decodes it and draws it, and the result matches the original. A 4-player snapshot stays under the size limit.** *(Automated: the same file.)*

## Changes from device testing
- **m10.1:** the right stick turns the held piece by pushing it left or right (faster the further), instead of pointing it; the playtest setting is now "Right-stick dead zone" (15%). Each Easy puzzle has two pieces to turn (House: roof and door; Rocket: both fins; Tree and sun: mountain and trunk), up from one. User: "With the analog stick, it's very difficult to rotate the piece smoothly" and "only one piece needs to be rotated for easy puzzles. This is too easy."
- **m10.2:** the phone works the results card: D-pad or left stick moves between Play again and Back, A presses, B goes back; the phone shows A "OK" and B "Back" there. Also fixed: after Play again the old game's clean-up put the phone back to its usual buttons for the new game. User: "I can't use the controller to select play again or back on the TV or Windows TV app."
- **m10.3:** on the results, pushing the left stick (or D-pad) up or down no longer takes the focus off the buttons, so A keeps working. The cause: the screen's own keyboard handler counted as a place the focus could move to. User: "If I move the stick on the sticks controller, the focus on play again is lost. If I press A, nothing happens. Pressing B does go back, however."
