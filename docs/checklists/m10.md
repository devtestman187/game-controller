# Milestone 10 checklist: Puzzle game for 1 player, with game-defined controls

Copied from the PRD v2 acceptance tests (M10). Both apps are **0.10.0**. Android phone and tablet checks stay **pending** (Android testing is on hold); the Android TV and Windows checks don't.

Test with the iPhone (TestFlight) and the Windows TV app (the zip on the GitHub release), or the Android TV (Play). No store forms change: the puzzle only adds more counts to the same anonymous session summary.

## What's new
- **TV: Play** on the home screen opens the game picker: **Shape Race** (new) and the **Arena**.
- **Shape Race**, 1 player: carry each piece from the tray, turn it with **X** and **Y** (or point it with the right stick in stick mode), and drop it on its outline with **A**. 3 Easy puzzles (House, Rocket, Tree and sun) and 1 Medium (Boat), 2 minutes each. A piece close enough to drop makes its outline light up and the phone tick; a right drop snaps and locks ("ka-CHUNK"); a wrong one turns red ("uh-oh"). At the end: the time, or the pieces placed, with **Play again**.
- **TV: Puzzle settings**: every tuning value (tolerances, speeds, delays, time limit), saved, with **Reset to defaults**. Changes count straight away.
- **Phone: the game's controls.** In the puzzle, A says "Pick up", X and Y say "Rotate", and the buttons the puzzle doesn't use are hidden. A small panel in the top bar shows the piece you're holding, turning as it turns, and the time left (red near the end). Back in the Arena, the usual buttons return.
- **Phone: new haptics** for snap, wrong, drop-ready, tick and help (help is for M11), each with its own strength in Settings.
- **Windows: keyboard player** works in the puzzle too: arrows, Space (pick up and drop), X and Z (rotate).
- Under the hood: the `layout` and `status` messages (docs/protocol.md §8a, §8b), games draw from snapshots into any box (S4, S8) and play sounds through the host (S5).

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] **An Easy puzzle can be finished with the D-pad, the gyro and the sticks.** Play → Shape Race → House (or any Easy), once in each mode (switch mode on the phone between games). *(Expect: "Done!" with a time each time.)*
- [ ] **A piece dropped within tolerance snaps exactly, locks and buzzes. A piece dropped in the wrong spot stays, turns red and buzzes differently. Both feel distinct on the phone.** *(Expect: a crisp click and a firm lock for the snap; a softer, falling "uh-oh" for the wrong drop, with the piece red and a ✗ on it. Pick a red piece up again and it turns back.)*
- [ ] **Changing the angle tolerance in playtest settings changes what counts as correct straight away.** Play → Puzzle settings → Angle tolerance down to ±2°, then play: a piece dropped a little crooked now turns red. Then up to ±30°: it snaps. Reset to defaults at the end.
- [ ] **Holding X and Y rotates smoothly in each direction, and the right stick points the piece in stick mode.** *(Expect: X turns clockwise ⟳, Y counter-clockwise ⟲, at an even speed while held; in stick mode the piece turns to face the way the right stick points.)*
- [X] **A square piece matches its outline at any quarter turn.** *(Automated: `rules_test.dart` drops a square at 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°. None of the four puzzles has a square, so on the phone:)* turn House's wall or door half a turn (upside down) and drop it. *(Expect: it snaps, since a rectangle matches at a half turn; the sun matches at any angle.)*
- [ ] **Time-up shows the pieces placed, and a finished puzzle shows the time.** Let one run out. *(Expect: "Time's up!" with "2 of 4 pieces placed" or similar; **Play again** has the remote's focus, so OK starts another.)*
- [ ] **The arena game is still available from the game picker.** Play → Arena: plays as before.
- [ ] **In the puzzle the phone shows "Pick up" on A and "Rotate" on X and Y, hides buttons the puzzle doesn't use, and shows the held piece turning as it rotates. Back on the arena game, the phone returns to its usual buttons.** Check in D-pad, Gyro and Sticks. *(Expect: B is a faint outline, Start and Select gone; in stick mode LB and RB gone too. The panel shows the time left with a bar, and the piece you hold.)*
- [ ] **With labels and the status panel showing, no control is smaller than before in any mode.** Compare with the Arena in each mode. *(Also checked by a test in each mode.)*
- [X] **A widget test draws the puzzle at 1280 by 720 in a box smaller than the window and at TV size, and the layout is the same apart from scale. The puzzle's sounds still play, through the host interface.** *(Automated: `packages/puzzle_game/test/view_test.dart`.)*
- [X] **A unit test encodes a puzzle snapshot, decodes it and draws it, and the result matches the original. A 4-player snapshot stays under the size limit.** *(Automated: the same file.)*

## Extra checks (not in the PRD, quick)
- [ ] Quick taps: tap A very briefly to pick up and drop several times. *(Expect: none are missed. Found on the emulator and fixed: the TV now keeps a tap held for at least 50 ms.)*
- [ ] Old with new: the M9 phone (TestFlight build 65) with the M10 TV plays the puzzle (no labels, no panel, the usual buttons), and the M10 phone with an M9 TV plays the Arena as before.
- [ ] Puzzle settings with the remote on the Android TV: move between rows, change a value, Reset to defaults.
- [ ] After a few puzzles, Stats → **Export**: the session file has a `gameStats` part with `puzzle` values (completion time, pieces placed, wrong drops, angle and position errors). The Stats screen itself shows the same rows as in M9.

### Optional, carried from M9
- [ ] *(optional)* TV first launch: the consent prompt appears once, "Don't share" has the remote's focus, and it doesn't come back after answering.
- [ ] *(optional)* Phone Settings → Control stats: turn it off, play a minute, and that phone's time doesn't grow on Stats.
- [ ] *(optional)* The rating prompt doesn't leave a button held, and answering it is one tap on a face.
