# Milestone 11 checklist: Shape Race for 2 to 4 players, and the whole TV app from a controller

Copied from the PRD v2 acceptance tests (M11). Both apps are **0.11.0**. Android phone and tablet checks stay **pending** (Android testing is on hold); the Android TV and Windows checks don't.

Test with the iPhone (TestFlight) and the Windows TV app (the zip on the GitHub release), or the Android TV (Play). Only the TV app changed; the phone app is the same as 0.10.0 apart from its version. With fewer than four phones, add simulated controllers on the TV's main screen (**Simulate controller**, then **Add simulated controller**, up to three): they join a side by themselves and move about at random. No store forms change.

## What's new
- **Join screen.** Play → Shape Race → a puzzle (or "Any … puzzle") now opens the join screen. Press **A** on the phone to join, then push **left or right** (D-pad, left stick, or tilt in gyro mode) to pick a side, at most two a side. **B** leaves your side, or goes back if you haven't joined. **Start** (or the remote's OK on Start) begins. One player, or two on the same side, play one board together; otherwise it's 1 vs 1, 2 vs 1 or 2 vs 2 on a split screen.
- **Split screen.** Each side has its own board and tray (the trays at the outer edges), with one clock and both sides' counts along the top. You can only play on your own side.
- **Big pieces.** One piece in each puzzle is big (House: the walls; Rocket: the body; Tree and sun: the crown; Boat: the hull). In a team, both teammates press A on it to carry it together at normal speed (it goes where you both push, on average; either of you can turn or drop it). One alone carries it slowly, its edge flashes toward the teammate, and the teammate's phone gives the **help** buzz every 3 seconds. A player alone on a side carries it at full speed like any piece.
- **Winning.** The first side to finish wins. At time-up the side with more pieces placed wins; equal pieces (including none) is a draw.
- **Results.** The winner (or "Draw!"), each side's time or pieces, and **Rematch** (Play again on one board), **Change teams** and **Back**.
- **Pause.** **Start** on any phone pauses for everyone, in Shape Race and the Arena. Only the player who paused, or Player 1, can resume (Start again, or Resume in the menu); **Quit** goes back to the game picker.
- **The whole TV app from a phone.** On every screen (home, game picker, puzzle list, Puzzle settings, Stats, the test tools), the D-pad or left stick moves the highlight, holding it keeps moving, **A** presses and **B** goes back. The phone shows A "OK" and B "Back" there. The remote still works as before.
- **Simulated controllers:** up to three at once (main screen).

## Acceptance tests
- [X] **Two players race side by side, and the first to finish wins with their time shown.** Two phones, one on each side, House or any Easy. *(Expect: "Left wins!" or "Right wins!", the winner's time in green, the other side's pieces placed; the winner's phone gives the success buzz; the other side stops moving.)*
- [X] **At time-up the side with more correct pieces wins, and a staged tie on pieces (including 0 vs 0) ends in a draw.** Set Puzzle settings → Time limit to 0:30 to save time. Once: one side places a piece more than the other. Then: nobody places anything (0 vs 0). Then: both sides place the same number. *(Expect: the side with more wins; both ties say "Draw!".)* Reset to defaults at the end.
- [ ] **Any player can pause with Start. Another player (not Player 1) can't resume someone else's pause, and Player 1 can resume any pause.** Three phones (simulated controllers never pause, so they can't stand in here). P2 presses Start: paused for everyone, the menu says "P2 or P1 can resume". P3 presses Start or A: still paused. P2 presses Start: resumed. P3 pauses; P1 presses A on Resume: resumed. *(Expect: nothing moves and the clock stops while paused; the phone shows Start "Resume".)*
- [ ] **In 2 vs 2, a big piece moves at normal speed with both teammates on it, slowly with one, and calls the other with an edge flash and a help haptic.** *(Expect: alone, the big piece crawls (about a third of normal speed), its edge facing your teammate flashes in their colour, and their phone buzzes every 3 s; once they press A on it too, it moves at normal speed where you both push and the calling stops; one press of A by either of you drops it.)* Two phones on one side and simulated controllers on the other are enough.
- [X] **In 2 vs 1, the solo player carries big pieces at full speed.** *(Expect: the player alone on their side carries the big piece like any other piece, with no flashing and no help buzz.)*
- [X] **Rematch keeps the teams, and Change teams goes back to the join screen.** *(Expect: Rematch starts another race with the same sides, a different puzzle if you chose "Any …"; Change teams opens the join screen with everyone still on their side.)*
- [ ] **Four controllers (phones or simulated) play a full match without lag, stuck input or disconnects.** A whole 2-minute 2 vs 2 match. *(Expect: dots follow the phones at once, no button stays held, nobody drops; Latency shows the usual input delay.)*
- [X] **From a phone alone, with the remote put away: leave a game through Start then Quit, pick another game and difficulty, change a Puzzle setting, open Stats, and go back to the home screen. In each mode, the stick and D-pad never leave the focus somewhere A can't press.** Do it once in D-pad, Gyro and Sticks mode. *(Expect: the highlight is always on a button; holding a direction keeps moving it; B always goes back one screen.)*

## Extra checks (not in the PRD, quick)
- [ ] A team of two on one board (both join the same side): the big piece needs both of you, and finishing shows "Done!" with the time.
- [ ] On the join screen with the remote: OK on Start begins; Back leaves.
- [ ] After any game ends or you quit, the phone shows A "OK" and B "Back" (not the game's buttons), and back in a game it shows the game's buttons again.
- [ ] A phone that didn't join watches: its buttons are hidden and its panel says "Watching".
- [ ] The Arena still plays as before, now with Start "Pause".

- **Automated:** 46 puzzle tests (16 new: formats, both sides' trays, players kept to their side, first to finish, time-up, draws, big pieces carried by two and by one, help calls, a solo player and the 2 vs 1 solo side at full speed, match snapshots under 2 KB) and 55 TV tests (9 new: the phone alone through the menus, held directions, simulated controllers ignored, the join screen, Start to begin, pause rules and Quit, the Arena's pause, race results with Rematch and Change teams, several simulated controllers); analyze clean.

## Changes from device testing
- **m11.1:** on the join screen, A (or Start) begins once you've joined, the phone shows A "Start", and the screen says "Press A or Start to begin" in large text. User: "After a player joins and picks a side, it's not stated what to press in order to start the game. Start worked, but A doesn't." The split screen draws the puzzle bigger: each board is cropped to its picture, and the tray goes beside or below it, whichever is bigger for that puzzle (House and Tree and sun about 69% of the one-board size, Rocket about 94% with the trays beside, Boat about 67% with the trays below and the clock between the boards; was 57%). User: "The puzzle is small in split screen."
- **m11.2:** only a player who joined can begin the match (Start from someone who hasn't joined does nothing), only players in the match can pause (not someone watching), and Start does nothing for the first half second of a game. User: "if player one joins and player 2 hasn't joined yet, player 2 can still press start to start the game. The game starts with 1 player (player 1) and it's paused." A tighter split screen, chosen by the owner from mock-ups: pieces start at the most compact allowed angles, and a long piece that starts correct lies along the tray (the Boat's mast: turn it 90° to place it); tray pieces are drawn at 75% and grow to full size when picked up; the pieces of a tray row overlap in a pile by up to 50%, never leaving one less than half visible; A picks up the top piece under your dot; a smaller clock, with the two boards side by side in the middle. Boards now about 99% (House), 124% (Rocket), 87% (Tree and sun) and 101% (Boat) of their one-board size, up from 57% in m11. User: "The puzzle pieces window is bigger than the puzzle itself. Can you try to stack and overlap the pieces instead to save screen space?" and "make the space between the two screens smaller … orienting the pieces in such a way that they take as little space as possible".
