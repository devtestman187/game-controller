## What's new
- **Touch metrics** on the phone, per input mode: presses, empty taps, near misses, slide-offs, rapid taps, stick and tilt times. Sent to the TV only, about every 10 s and on every mode switch, and kept while the Wi-Fi is off.
- **Settings → Control stats** on the phone: says what's recorded; a switch to turn it off.
- **"How did the controls feel?"** after some arena rounds (first after round 3, then at most every 5 rounds and 3 minutes): three faces, optional tags, Skip.
- **TV: Stats** button on the home screen: each session per phone and mode, **Export**, and **Sharing** on or off.
- **TV: consent prompt** on the first launch, for the adult. Nothing is shared until someone picks **Share stats**.
- **Latency screen**: the delay in stages (phone to TV, TV to the screen).
- **Windows: keyboard player**: in Test game, the arrow keys move and Space dashes, as another player.
- Under the hood: capabilities in hello/welcome, the game host interface and standard gamepad mapping, and the protocol spec in docs/protocol.md.
