# Milestone 12 checklist: phone-hosted play

Both apps are **0.12.0**. Build 1 (`m12.1`) had steps 1 to 5 (hosting on a phone). Build 2 (`m12.3`) added steps 6 to 8: another phone joins a phone-hosted game and sees it on its own screen, and the stats gain the host phone's battery and heat. Build 3 (`m12.4`) fixed the phone tray and moved the clock beside the board. **Build 4** is the owner's build 3 device-test fixes and four changes the owner asked for (below). The Android phone checks are no longer **pending** (owner, Nov 7); the TV and Windows checks wait until the owner has them at hand.

Install from TestFlight on the iPhone and the iPad.

## What's new in build 1
- **Host a game on the phone.** On the Connect screen, **Host a game**, then **Play on this phone**: the phone is the host and a controller at once. The game picker and the games draw behind your controls; the controls are see-through over a game.
- **Show on this device.** The other choice: the phone shows the games like a TV, with no controls on top, for others to join (joining comes in build 2).
- **The host chip** in the top bar: "Hosting · <code>", with the pairing code, the address, **New code** and **Stop hosting**. The back button stops hosting too.
- **Locking the phone** pauses the game. Back within 30 s, it goes on; after longer, the round ends and you're back at the game picker.
- **Settings → Game on this phone → Controls over the game:** how see-through the controls are over a game (20% to 100%, 60% at first).

## What's new in build 2
- **Other phones join.** A phone hosting a game shows in the others' Connect screen under **Games on this Wi-Fi**, as "Thumb Party on <phone name>" with a phone or tablet icon. Tapping it asks for the code, as with the TV; typing the code works too.
- **The game on the joined phone.** In **Play on this phone**, every phone draws its own side of the board behind its buttons, with your count ("You 2/4") and the other side's. In **Show on this device**, the host device draws the whole match.
- **Waiting for the host.** Locking the host phone pauses the round for up to 30 s; the other phones say **Waiting for the host to come back** and count down. Past 30 s the round ends and they say why.
- **A compact top bar over a game**: the mode menu and the app buttons at the controls' opacity, with the host's chip and code while hosting.
- **Settings → Game on this phone → Frame rate (test):** 60, 30 or 20 frames a second from the next round. Fewer frames save battery but look less smooth.
- **Diagnostics → Session summary:** while hosting and until the app closes, the host's session in a form you can send for the report.

## What's new in build 4
- **A player who locks mid-round pauses it** for up to 30 s. The host's screen says "P2 is away" with a countdown, **Go on without them** and **End round**; every other joined phone says "Player 2 is away" with the countdown. Back in time, the round goes on from where it stopped; after 30 s it goes on without them.
- **A joined phone can always leave.** The waiting card has **Leave game**; it says "Reconnecting to the game…" when the link dropped, "Waiting for the host to come back" when the host locked, and **The host has left** once the wait is out. Over a game, the dot in the top bar opens the Connect screen.
- **Back on the results goes to the game picker** (the Back button, B, the swipe), on the phone and the TV. A new round only from Play again or Rematch. The phone's swipe-back stops hosting only from the picker.
- **The host phone's menus are touch-only.** No pad on the picker, the join screen, the results or the pause menu; the pad comes up for the round. On the join screen the host's player is already on a side ("(you)"); tap the other side to switch.
- **"Get ready" 3, 2, 1** before each round on a phone host; the clock waits, and the joined phones already show the board.
- **The other player's mirror** in a race, top left: their board, their orange dot (on the top edge while they're at their tray) and the piece they hold. Too narrow a phone for a wide puzzle keeps the count only.
- **The host chip shows the Wi-Fi address**, never mobile data or Wi-Fi Calling's.
- **The host log** says when a round starts, ends, pauses and resumes, and what was pressed on the results.
