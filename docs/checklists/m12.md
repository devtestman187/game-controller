# Milestone 12 checklist: phone-hosted play

Both apps are **0.12.0** (tag `m12`, test build 1). M12 isn't finished: this build has steps 1 to 5 (hosting on a phone). Joining a phone-hosted game from another phone (steps 6 to 8) comes in the next build, and step 9 copies the PRD's M12 acceptance tests in here. Android checks stay **pending** while Android testing is on hold; the TV and Windows checks wait until the owner has them at hand (owner, Nov 7).

Install from TestFlight on the iPhone and the iPad.

## What's new in build 1
- **Host a game on the phone.** On the Connect screen, **Host a game**, then **Play on this phone**: the phone is the host and a controller at once. The game picker and the games draw behind your controls; the controls are see-through over a game.
- **Show on this device.** The other choice: the phone shows the games like a TV, with no controls on top, for others to join (joining comes in build 2).
- **The host chip** in the top bar: "Hosting · <code>", with the pairing code, the address, **New code** and **Stop hosting**. The back button stops hosting too.
- **Locking the phone** pauses the game. Back within 30 s, it goes on; after longer, the round ends and you're back at the game picker.
- **Settings → Game on this phone → Controls over the game:** how see-through the controls are over a game (20% to 100%, 60% at first).

## Build 1 checks
- [ ] **A solo Shape Race on the iPhone with Wi-Fi off.** Wi-Fi and mobile data off. Host a game, Play on this phone, Shape Race, any Easy puzzle, join with A, start, and finish it. *(Expect: the picker and the board behind your controls; your dot moves with the D-pad or stick; pieces snap; the results show your time; haptics as usual.)*
- [ ] **Lock and unlock within 30 s: the round goes on.** During a round, lock the phone for about 10 s and unlock. *(Expect: the game was paused (the clock didn't move) and goes on by itself, no pause menu.)*
- [ ] **Lock for over 30 s: the round ends.** Lock for about 40 s and unlock. *(Expect: back at the game picker; hosting still runs.)*
- [ ] **The Arena on the host phone.** Host, pick the Arena, play a round. *(Expect: your player moves; dash, wall hits and the goal buzz.)*
- [ ] **Sounds play on the host phone.** In both games. *(Expect: the Bubble Pop sounds, as on the TV. Not yet checked anywhere: the PC sandbox blocks them.)*
- [ ] **Controls over the game.** Settings → Game on this phone, set 20%, then 100%, during a game. *(Expect: the controls fade and come back; they don't move or shrink, and touching between them doesn't press anything.)*
- [ ] **Show on this device.** Host a game, Show on this device. *(Expect: the picker fills the screen with no controls; the host chip shows the code.)*
- [ ] **Stop hosting.** From the chip's menu, and once with the back button. *(Expect: back to the Connect screen; Host a game works again.)*
- [ ] **The iPad sees the iPhone's game.** iPhone hosting with Wi-Fi on, the iPad on the same Wi-Fi, on its Connect screen. *(Expect: the iPhone's game in the iPad's host list as "Thumb Party on iPhone …". Joining it isn't in this build.)* Then lock the iPhone for 10 s, unlock, and look again. *(Expect: still listed. If it's gone, the phone didn't restart its connection after the lock; tell Claude.)*
- [ ] **The phone as a controller still works.** With a TV or PC when one is at hand: pending until then.
