# Milestone 12 checklist: phone-hosted play

Both apps are **0.12.0** (test build 2; build 1 was tagged `m12`). Build 1 had steps 1 to 5 (hosting on a phone). This build adds steps 6 to 8: another phone joins a phone-hosted game and sees the game on its own screen, and the stats and the report gain the host phone's battery and heat. Step 9 is these docs. The Android phone checks are no longer **pending** (owner, Nov 7); the TV and Windows checks wait until the owner has them at hand.

Install from TestFlight on the iPhone and the iPad.

## What's new in build 1
- **Host a game on the phone.** On the Connect screen, **Host a game**, then **Play on this phone**: the phone is the host and a controller at once. The game picker and the games draw behind your controls; the controls are see-through over a game.
- **Show on this device.** The other choice: the phone shows the games like a TV, with no controls on top, for others to join (joining comes in build 2).
- **The host chip** in the top bar: "Hosting · <code>", with the pairing code, the address, **New code** and **Stop hosting**. The back button stops hosting too.
- **Locking the phone** pauses the game. Back within 30 s, it goes on; after longer, the round ends and you're back at the game picker.
- **Settings → Game on this phone → Controls over the game:** how see-through the controls are over a game (20% to 100%, 60% at first).

## What's new in build 2
- **Other phones join.** A phone hosting a game shows in the others' Connect screen under **Games on this Wi-Fi**, as "Thumb Party on <phone name>" with a phone or tablet icon. Tapping it asks for the code, as with the TV; typing the code works too.
- **The game on the joined phone.** In **Play on this phone**, every phone draws its own side of the board behind its buttons, with your count ("You 2/4") and the other side's. In **Show on this device**, the host device draws the whole match.
- **Waiting for the host.** Locking the host phone pauses the round for up to 30 s; the other phones say **Waiting for the host to come back** and count down. Past 30 s the round ends and they say why.
- **A compact top bar over a game**: the mode menu and the app buttons at the controls' opacity, with the host's chip and code while hosting.
- **Settings → Game on this phone → Frame rate (test):** 60, 30 or 20 frames a second from the next round. Fewer frames save battery but look less smooth.
- **Diagnostics → Session summary:** while hosting and until the app closes, the host's session in a form you can send for the report.

## Build 2 checks
- [ ] **Two phones, one game.** The iPhone hosting (**Host a game**, **Play on this phone**) and the Android phone (or the iPad) on the same Wi-Fi. On the other phone's Connect screen, tap the iPhone under **Games on this Wi-Fi**, then type the code. *(Expect: the iPhone appears as a Phone, "Thumb Party on <the iPhone's name>", with a phone icon; the code pairs it as usual; the game lists the new player.)*
- [ ] **A Shape Race race on both phones.** Shape Race, any Easy puzzle, one player on each side, start. *(Expect: the joined phone shows its own side's board and tray behind its buttons, its "You 1/5" and the other side's count; each phone moves only its own dot; the winner's time shows on both.)*
- [ ] **The Arena on both phones.** *(Expect: on the joined phone, the Arena behind its buttons with its own dot and the goal; dash, wall hits and the goal buzz on the phone that caused them.)*
- [ ] **Sounds on both phones.** A round of each game. *(Expect: the Bubble Pop sounds on the host phone and on the joined phone.)*
- [ ] **Lock the host for about 10 s during a round.** *(Expect: the joined phone says **Waiting for the host to come back** and counts down ("The game goes on if they're back in 28 s"); unlock, and the round goes on from where it stopped with the clock where it was.)*
- [ ] **Lock the host for about 40 s during a round.** *(Expect: the round ends; the joined phone says **The host was away too long, so the round ended.**; the host phone is back at the game picker, still hosting.)*
- [ ] **The top bar over a game, and the host chip.** While hosting on the iPhone and playing a round. *(Expect: the compact bar along the top (mode menu, Settings, Diagnostics) at the buttons' opacity, not the full one; the host's chip with the pairing code is on it.)*
- [ ] **Frame rate (test): 30, then 20.** Settings → Game on this phone → Frame rate (test): set 30, play a round; set 20, play a round; back to 60. *(Expect: the picture moves in bigger steps at 30 and 20 but the buttons respond at once; no dragging or piling up.)*
- [ ] **The iPad joins.** The iPad on the same Wi-Fi, joining the iPhone's **Play on this phone** game. *(Expect: the iPad shows the whole match, both sides cropped to the puzzle, behind its own buttons.)*
- [ ] **The iPad as the screen.** The iPad hosting (**Show on this device**) with both phones joining it. *(Expect: the whole match on the iPad with no buttons on it, and its sounds; each phone is a plain controller, with no game behind its buttons, as with a TV.)*
- [ ] **Send the Diagnostics to Claude.** Copy the joined phone's **Game view** line, and the host phone's **Session summary**, into the message with the results. *(Expect: the joined phone's line has its frame timing for the rounds; the host's summary has a line per round with the frame rate, where each player watched, and the phone's battery and heat.)*
