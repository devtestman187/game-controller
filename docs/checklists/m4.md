# Milestone 4 checklist: Analog stick mode

Copied from the PRD acceptance tests, with your answers applied: Start and Select instead of View and Menu, no triggers, fixed **and** floating sticks with a toggle, no stick click. Run it with the `m4` builds: the iPhone `.ipa` from Codemagic (tag `m4`), the TV APK (0.4.0) and, if you like, the Windows TV app. Android phone items are marked *(deferred)*.

Setup: open **Controller Test** on the TV and **Controller** on the iPhone (it reconnects). At the top of the phone, tap **Sticks**. The layout:
- **LB** top left, **RB** top right
- **Left stick** upper left, with the **D-pad** below it toward the middle
- **A/B/X/Y** upper right, with the **right stick** below it toward the middle
- **Select** and **Start** in the middle (Select on the left)

The tune icon opens the stick settings: **Floating sticks** on/off, dead zone and sensitivity, all saved. On the TV, the controller's picture switches to the same layout, with "L x, y   R x, y" values.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] **Each stick moves the TV indicator smoothly, and letting go returns it to center (iPhone).** Do this with fixed sticks and again with **Floating sticks** on. In floating mode, put your thumb anywhere in the left or right half (not on a button) and the stick starts there.
- [ ] *(deferred)* The same on the Android phone.
- [ ] **Both sticks and a button at once.** Move both sticks with your thumbs while holding LB or RB with a finger. *(Expect: the TV shows both sticks and the shoulder button together.)*
- [ ] **Never past full scale, and the dead zone.** Push a stick fully into a diagonal. *(Expect: the TV values stay within the circle, about 0.71 / 0.71 at most, never 1 / 1.)* Tune icon → raise **Dead zone**: small thumb movements around the center stay at 0.00.
- [ ] **Every button shows and ticks.** A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, Select, Start and the four D-pad directions. *(Expect: each lights on the TV and ticks on the phone; moving a stick alone never ticks.)*
- [ ] **Switching modes and nothing stuck.** Switch among D-pad, Gyro and Sticks mid-session. *(Expect: no reconnect.)* Also hold a stick and a button, then (a) switch mode, (b) swipe home, (c) turn Wi-Fi off. *(Expect: in every case the TV shows everything released and the sticks centered.)*
- [ ] **The layout fits.** Nothing overlaps or sits under the screen's rounded corners or camera cut-out on the iPhone. *(Android phone: deferred.)*

## Extra checks (not in the PRD, quick)
- [ ] Higher **Sensitivity** reaches full scale with less thumb travel. The settings are kept after relaunching the app.
- [ ] A fixed stick ignores touches just outside its circle. A floating stick ignores touches on the buttons around it.
- [ ] The TV's **Simulate controller** (and `sim_controller --demo`) now ends each loop with both sticks circling and LB, RB and Y pressed.
- [ ] Diagnostics → Input shows the mode, the live stick values and the stick settings.
