# Milestone 7 checklist: Bigger buttons, tablets and Android catch-up

Copied from the PRD v2 acceptance tests (M7). Run it with controller **0.7.0**: the APK on the Android phone and the Android tablet, and the `m7` iPhone `.ipa` from Codemagic on the iPhone and the iPad (iLoader, for the last time). The TV app is unchanged (0.6.0), so the TV and the Windows app stay as they are.

Setup:
- **Android phone and tablet**: install `controller_app/build/app/outputs/flutter-apk/app-release.apk` (see docs/building.md, or ask me to install it over adb). It replaces 0.6.0 and keeps the pairing.
- **iPhone and iPad**: install the `m7` `.ipa` with iLoader. On the iPad it's a new install, so enter the TV's code once.
- Open **Controller Test** on the TV (or the Windows app).

What's new:
- **Stick mode**: A, B, X and Y are much bigger, about 75% of their D-pad-mode size (on an iPhone 15-size screen, 62 px across instead of 34). They sit closer together in the top right, with the right stick about 20% smaller below and toward the middle. The left side is unchanged.
- **Button size** (stick settings, the tune icon, and Settings → Sticks): 60% to 100% of D-pad mode, default 75%. On a small screen the largest sizes are limited so nothing overlaps (the right stick gives way first, down to 60% of the left one).
- **Touch areas**: in stick mode, each of A, B, X and Y takes touches up to 15% beyond its drawn circle. Between two buttons the nearer one wins, and the corners of the buttons' square are left to the right stick.
- **Tablets**: controls are sized in millimetres and stay thumb-sized in the bottom corners, leaving the middle free. A fresh install starts in stick mode on a tablet. The TV now lists the device as "iPad …" or "Android tablet …".
- **Windows**: the controller stays in landscape on tablets (Android: declared as a game; iPad: landscape only, with the iPadOS 26 orientation lock and a minimum window size). Windows that are too small show **Make the window bigger**.
- **Without haptics** (Settings → Haptics → *Without haptics*): **Flash on press** (on by itself on devices with no vibration, like every iPad) and an optional **Click sound**.
- **Android 12**: the controller now installs on Android 12 and later (was 16).

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] **In stick mode, A, B, X and Y are clearly larger, about 75% of their D-pad-mode size, and nothing overlaps on either phone.** Switch between **D-pad** and **Sticks** to compare. *(Expect: in stick mode the buttons look about three quarters the size they are in D-pad mode, and no control touches another or sits under a rounded corner or the camera cut-out.)*
- [ ] **The button size setting changes the size live, stays within its range without overlaps, and is saved between launches.** Tune icon → drag **Button size** from 60% to 100% while watching the buttons around the dialog. Close it, check nothing overlaps at 100%, then swipe the app away and reopen it. *(Expect: the size changes as you drag, never overlaps the right stick, and is the same after reopening.)*
- [ ] **A press just outside a button's drawn edge still registers, and never triggers a neighbouring control.** In stick mode, press just outside the outer edge of each of A, B, X and Y, then just outside B on the side toward Y. *(Expect: the TV shows the button you were nearest, never two at once. Pressing in the empty corner below-left of X does nothing, or moves the right stick if it's under your thumb.)*
- [ ] **Both sticks, the D-pad, LB, RB, Select and Start still work with two thumbs at once.** Hold the left stick and the right stick, then add a D-pad direction or Start with another finger, and LB or RB. *(Expect: the TV shows all of them together, and everything returns to centre when you let go.)*
- [ ] **Every v1 checklist item marked "Android phone pending" passes on the Android phone.** See the list below.
- [ ] **On the iPad and the Android tablet, the controls are thumb-sized and within reach of both thumbs while holding the tablet, in all three modes.** Hold the tablet in landscape with both hands. *(Expect: in D-pad, Gyro and Sticks modes you can reach every control without letting go. The middle of the screen stays empty.)*
- [ ] **The controller stays in landscape on both tablets with rotation lock off, and a resized or split-screen window still works or shows "Make the window bigger".**
  - Turn rotation lock off and turn the tablet to portrait and back. *(Expect: the controller stays in landscape.)*
  - Android tablet: open it in split screen next to another app, then drag the divider. *(Expect: it works at half the screen; when it gets too narrow it shows "Make the window bigger", and works again when you widen it.)*
  - iPad: in windowed mode, drag the window's corner smaller and larger. *(Expect: the same. iPadOS stops the window at a minimum size.)*
- [ ] **On the iPad, presses flash and the optional click sound plays, and the app runs without errors with no haptics hardware.** Press buttons and D-pad directions. Then Settings → *Without haptics* → turn on **Click sound** (the iPad must not be muted). *(Expect: each press flashes white briefly. With the click on, each press and new direction clicks. Settings → Haptics says "This device has no haptics".)*
- [ ] **On the Android 12 emulator, the controller installs, pairs with the TV app and sends input.** *(I run this one on the PC; see the result below.)*
- [ ] **Both store accounts are applied for.** The Apple Developer Program ($99 a year) and the Google Play developer account ($25 once). Tell me when each is approved; M8 needs both.

- Automated: 229 phone tests, including 105 layout tests: every control on screen with no overlaps at every button size (60% to 100%) on 15 screens (the two test phones' sizes, an iPhone SE, a small 16:9 phone, the smallest window, an 11-inch and a 13-inch iPad, an iPad mini, an 11-inch and an 8-inch Android tablet, an unfolded foldable, and half-screen split windows on both tablets); A/B/X/Y at 75% of D-pad mode by default; their touch areas never reaching the right stick's; D-pad mode unchanged on phones; tablets keeping controls in the bottom corners at the same physical size on iPad and Android. Widget tests: the size setting changes live and is saved, presses just outside a button count and go to the nearest one, the square's corners take no touches, two thumbs and a finger at once, stick mode by default on a tablet, "Make the window bigger" lets go of held buttons, the smallest window has no overflow, the flash and the click.
