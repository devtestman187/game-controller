# Party ideas (draft)

Ideas to make Thumb Party feel like a family party game, in the spirit of Mario Party and Lego Party. **Status: planned (Nov 8, 2026).** The owner: "Yes let's plan for this content in M14." The PRD's party requirements G1 to G8 and its M14 (Party night: points, party nights, the cat, awards) and M15 (power-ups, more chaos, the hall of fame) were approved by the owner on Nov 8, 2026 ("Approve M14 with your latest rules"); the cat's rules there replace section 1's (on Party it may knock one placed piece loose, within limits). The sections below are the ideas as first written.

The owner liked sections 1 to 3, including party nights and end-of-night awards (2026-11-03). Section 4 was raised but not chosen.

Ground rules:
- Use genre ideas only (rounds, chaos, bonus awards). Characters, names and art are our own, never another game's.
- Leaderboards stay on the device that hosted the game (PRD v2: no online leaderboards; Families policy).
- Chaos must feel fair to an 8-year-old: always warned before it happens, never undoes finished work.

## 1. Chaos events

Random surprises during a Shape Race round.

**The cat**
- Warned first: a paw appears at the edge of the screen and a "meow" plays.
- Walks across one side and bats a loose piece somewhere else: a tray piece, or an unplaced one on the board.
- Never touches locked pieces.
- Players can shoo it: move your dot onto it and press A, and it runs off.
- Picks on the side that's ahead (catch-up), so rounds stay close.

**Other events** (one at a time, a few seconds each)
- Gust of wind: loose pieces drift a little sideways.
- Lights out: the board goes dark except a spotlight around each player's dot.
- Wobble: the held piece wiggles, or rotation briefly runs backwards (Medium and Hard only).
- Piece shuffle: the tray pieces swap places.
- Bubble float: one piece floats away slowly and has to be caught.

**Chaos setting** in Puzzle settings: Off / A little / Party, default "A little". The playtest compares the three settings using the frustration signals the game already records.

## 2. Power-ups

A star now and then appears in a side's tray. Picking it up with A gives that side a random bonus. The phone shows what you got (with a buzz) and when it's used.

- Magnet: the next drop snaps from twice as far away.
- Hint glow: one outline lights up for its piece.
- Send the cat: the cat goes to the other side.
- Freeze: the other side's dots slow down for 3 seconds.
- Time bonus: +10 seconds for your side.

## 3. Points, party nights and leaderboards

**Points in a round** (shown as big pop-up numbers)

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Piece snapped on the first try | +2 |
| Piece snapped after a wrong drop | +1 |
| Finishing first | +5 |
| Helping carry a big piece | +1 to each helper |
| Shooing the cat | +1 |

**Party night:** a set of 3 or 5 rounds, with the scoreboard between rounds. The last round is worth double points, so a team that's behind can still win.

**End-of-night awards:** a few random awards from a list, each with bonus points, revealed one at a time like a show. Examples: Cat Catcher, Best Helper, Sharpshooter (fewest wrong drops), Speedy Snapper. The winner isn't certain until the end, and players who lost the races can still win an award.

**Leaderboards** (stored only on the TV or host phone)
- Tonight's scoreboard: the running scores for the party night.
- Hall of fame: best times per puzzle (already planned for M13, T15), plus all-time party wins and awards by nickname.
- Players are known by nickname (C20, planned for M13), so nicknames would move up with this work.

## 4. Raised, not chosen

- Mini-games between puzzles, built on the Arena's movement: grab the most coins, tag, stay in the circle.
- Characters: each player picks an original animal avatar instead of a numbered dot. (Nov 8, 2026: M13 gives each player a colour and an accessory, designed so they can be chosen later; PRD L6 and L7.)
- Reactions: tap an emoji on the phone and it pops up on the TV beside your dot.
