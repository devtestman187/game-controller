# m16.1 build summary (0.14.0, build 981, tag on commit 6272d949)

Changes since m14.9. Source: git log m14.9..m16.1 (136 commits, mostly the phone-host layout and its review runs), docs/briefs/m16/STATUS.md, docs/HANDOFF.md section 7, docs/code-health.md.

## 1. Custom player avatars

What exists today (already shipped before m16, from M14):
- A player picks a colour (1 of 6) and an accessory (ears, antenna, bow, cap, tuft, crown) in the phone's Settings, with a preview. Code: controller_app/lib/src/ui/settings_profile_section.dart, controller_app/lib/src/profile.dart, controller_app/lib/src/providers/profile.dart. Types: packages/protocol/lib/src/character.dart.
- The player also types a nickname (up to 10 characters). It is saved on the phone and sent when joining.
- The host gives each player a colour and accessory nobody else wears. If the pick is taken, the player gets the nearest free one, and the phone says so. The host draws the result on the TV and phone-host screens (player_badge.dart, join_side_panel.dart, score_row.dart in packages/party_host; toy_character.dart in packages/toy_look).
- Default for a player who never picks: player 1 gets colour 1 with ears, player 2 colour 2 with the antenna, and so on.

Not available: picture or photo avatars, free colour choice, more than 6 colours and 6 accessories. m16.1 adds no avatar change.

Planned, not built: M16 item 5 "identity" comes after the phone-host layout, on the owner's go. As written in the PRD it covers nickname, colour and accessory on the phone shown on the TV, a stable ID and a spoken name for each of the 36 combinations (such as "the Red Bow") for Thumby, and a future path to more colours and accessories. Much of the picking already exists, so the new work is mostly the IDs and spoken names, plus any join-flow polish.

What a tester sees: Settings has nickname, colour and accessory. After joining, the TV shows a coloured character with the accessory beside each name. A taken pick is swapped for the nearest free one.

## 2. New features and visible changes since m14.9

Phone controller (joined phone):
- The join screen shows the 4-digit pairing code.
- The phone prefers a host found live on the network over the stale remembered host. Reconnect waits up to 1 second for discovery.
- A round strip over the pad shows "Round N of M", a clock chip, the phone's own coins and its place ("1st", "2nd"). This needed a new optional protocol field. Older apps still work.
- A "host is away" card appears when the TV or host phone is backgrounded.
- Contrast fixes on the pad: Ready and +43 text, D-pad centre dot, unused face circles, state dot, stick knob edge.
- When the host phone is also a player, the framed boards' pad clearance is corrected (it sat 52 device px too low).

TV / host (also the phone when it hosts):
- All party-night screens are redrawn in one scaled 16:9 "host frame" with no scrolling, a footer bar and a 20 px minimum text size. They are: night start (with award tiles), scoreboard, final scores, awards reveal, Arena results, puzzle results, game picker and join screen.
- The TV wears the same frame, at 2x on 1920x1080. A test checks the TV focus ring.
- The lobby holds a dropped player's seat. The row is dimmed and marked "... is away". The player returns to the same side, and the round is not paused for a lobby player who is not in it.
- Night footer buttons are sized by their labels. Six-player names are split into two rows of three. Five players are laid out 3 + 2. Reveal tie names shrink and split into two columns.
- The join hint is hidden on touch hosts. Dead night widgets were removed.

Windows:
- No Windows-specific feature work was found. Windows gets the host-frame screens and the host-away card (the TV app installs the same lifecycle watcher). Windows zip built for m16.1.

Internal (not visible): test/PNG hygiene, review runs, docs.

## 3. Bugs fixed since m14.9
- Reconnect ignored a host that discovery had already found (item 3).

- Six contrast failures below 3:1 (item 4).
- Pad clearance on the framed board was 52 device px too low.
- Clipped or ellipsised labels: "Bumper Round" and "Change teams" in the footer, six-player night names, puzzle results controls.
- The tablet-board test rewrote its PNG on every run (test hygiene).
- No numbered tracker rows (B-xxx) were closed in this window. m14.9's B-031 passed its device check.
