# M13 4a: Shape Race drawn in the toy look

Shape Race no longer draws in M12's dark colours: the board, the tray, the sky, the targets, the pieces, the dots, the
HUD and the mirror are all drawn from the `ToyLook` the view finds above it, on the host and on a joined phone alike,
and the snap and the wrong drop boing and wobble from the snapshots.

## What changed

### packages/puzzle_game

- `pubspec.yaml`: `toy_look` as a path dependency (`path: ../toy_look`), as `party_host` has it. Ran `flutter pub get` at
  the repository root.
- `lib/puzzle_game.dart`: exports `src/piece_moments.dart`.
- `lib/src/piece_moments.dart` (new, 1 file): a rules-side helper with no Flutter or toy_look import. It is fed every new
  snapshot with the time it arrived, notes when a piece turns `locked` or `wrong` (per board, so a match's two sides are
  kept apart), clears on a new round (a new puzzle id), and answers how long ago that was, plus the boing's scale, the
  wobble's angle and the sparkle's grow/left, all still with `effects: false`.
- `lib/src/view.dart`: `PuzzleView` and `MatchView` are stateful now. Each keeps one `PieceMoments` and an injectable
  clock (a `Stopwatch` started at build by default), observes each new snapshot in `initState`/`didUpdateWidget`, and
  hands `ToyLookScope.of(context)` to its painter. `paint` is not ticked: the game sends a snapshot every frame.
- `lib/src/puzzle_painter.dart`: every colour is a `ToyColors` token (bar `Color(spec.color)`, the puzzle's data). One
  `_Styles` per paint holds the look's ink, its outline and its shadow divided by the field's scale, the held piece's
  shadow twice as far and the wrong drop's edge in `bad`. The board's mat and the tray strip, the pieces and the pillows
  are drawn with `toyShape` and `look.shade`; the targets are a dashed ink outline with a 0.25 fill (the piece's colour on
  Easy, `targetNeutral` otherwise, `good`/`focus` for ready/hint); the marks are `good` and `bad` with an ink rim; the dot
  is `paintToyBadge` at today's dot size; the HUD's text is the look's clock and button families in `onBackground`, the
  warning clock sits on a `bad` pill in ink, and the time bar is a `good` fill (else `bad`) on a `paper` track with the ink
  edge.
- `lib/src/board_hud.dart` (new), `lib/src/match_hud.dart` (new), `lib/src/text_paint.dart` (new): the two painters were
  split along their HUD and text seams so no lib file passes 400 lines (381, 357, 100, 71 and 91 lines).
- `lib/src/match_painter.dart`: the field behind is the look's flat `background`; the phone HUD panel is paper at 0.9
  with the ink edge and ink text; the strip and the gap HUD take the look's type and its `onBackground`, the bar its
  `good`/`bad` on `paper`; the mirror is drawn by `MirrorPainter`.
- `lib/src/mirror_painter.dart`: the mirror's frame is the ink edge with the other side's colour (`player(2)`) as the band
  inside it, its board flat in `board`, its dots `paper` with the ink edge, its pieces in their own colours.
- `lib/src/camera.dart`: `_glyphEm` is re-measured and becomes the public `PuzzleCamera.glyphEm = 0.55`; camera.dart still
  imports no toy_look.
- `test/support/look.dart` (new): the Sunny and Night looks the tests draw in, and `withLook`, which puts a `ToyLookScope`
  above a view as both apps do.
- `test/support/look_fonts.dart` (new): loads the look's Fredoka and Nunito from `../toy_look/fonts`, as `party_host`'s
  `toy_fonts.dart` does.
- `test/look_paint_test.dart` (new, 5 tests): the board's middle, the tray's middle and the sky outside the board are the
  look's tokens in Sunny and in Night; an Easy target's fill is its colour at 0.25 over the board; a locked piece's middle
  is its colour (shade allowed); the warning clock's pill is `bad` with ink text, and nothing is `bad` without it.
- `test/piece_moments_test.dart` (new, 10 tests): noted with the time, the first snapshot suppressed, the age growing,
  boards apart, a new puzzle clearing, out-of-range pieces, the boing at 0/75/150 ms, the wobble at 0/30/60/90/120 ms,
  the sparkle, effects off.
- `test/text_measure_test.dart` (new, 2 tests): measures the HUD's real strings in Fredoka 600 and checks `glyphEm`
  against the widest per character and the 15 % ceiling.
- `test/view_test.dart`, `test/support/frames.dart`, `test/support/mirror_helpers.dart`, `test/mirror_test.dart`,
  `test/mirror_place_test.dart`, `test/camera_test.dart`: the look wired in and their colour/form pins moved (below).

## The text measure (rule 5)

`PuzzleCamera.glyphEm` was 0.5. Measured with Fredoka 600, as `text_measure_test.dart` does (width / size / length, in
em per character):

| text | em/char | | text | em/char |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `88:88` | 0.4826 | | `Them 3/6` | **0.5474** |
| `0:00` | 0.4852 | | `Them 0/12` | 0.5348 |
| `You 0/4` | 0.4996 | | `0/4` | 0.5397 |
| `Right 0/4` | 0.4693 | | `You 0/12` | 0.4885 |
| `You 0/4 · Right 0/4` | 0.4426 | | `You 0/12 · Right 0/12` | 0.4396 |
| `Left 0/4 · Right 0/4` | 0.4295 | | `Left 0/12 · Right 0/12` | 0.4278 |
| `Left 0/6 · Right 0/6` | 0.4272 | | `0 of 4 placed` | 0.4468 |
| `0 of 12 placed` | 0.4443 | | | |

The widest is the mirror's short line "Them 3/6" at 0.5474, so 0.5 was **under** it: the form chosen for that text could
be narrower than the text. `glyphEm` is now **0.55**: 0.5 % over "Them 3/6", 14 % over the clock "88:88" — neither over
the 15 % ceiling (the clock is the wider ratio) and neither under the real width. `text_measure_test` pins both.

## Pixels, and the scale test

Under rule 2 every look size is divided by the field's scale, so two renders of the same layout at different logical
px/unit differ along every edge (a 3 px outline in a frame captured at 2/3 is 2 px, and its shadow shifts). The scale
test in `view_test.dart` therefore pins what the layout owns — the board's left edge, the tray's right edge and the time
bar's top edge, each within 1 px between the two frames — and allows the whole-frame mean difference 3.0 (it was 2.0 and
measured 2.42 with the look's edges). The frames' sizes and the layout are still identical.

## Section 5's Shape Race bullet: what is not done

- **The dotted, drifting sky** is step 6; the field behind is the look's flat `background`, as the brief says.
- **The strip and gap time bars** take the look's `paper`/`good`/`bad` but not its ink edge: they are 0.5–0.8 world units
  thin, and the edge at that size fills the bar rather than outlining it.
- **The mirrored count is not orange**: its words are `onBackground` (or ink on the panel) like the rest of the HUD, and
  the other side's colour shows in the mirror's frame and the dots, per D12 (text is always ink).
- **The held piece** shows the same ink edge as a resting one, with its shadow twice as far; no second outline.

## Self-check, review rules part A

1. **Every host setup.** The same `PuzzleView`/`MatchView` and the same painters draw on the TV/PC host, a phone on "Play
   on this phone", a phone on "Show on this device" and a joined phone: none reads a platform, a size or a host (rule B5).
   `view_test`'s scale test and `look_paint_test` pump the views at 640 × 360 and at TV size, and `mirror_test`/
   `mirror_place_test` cover the phone's own side. `puzzle_module_test`/`puzzle_session_test` still pass, so an older
   build's protocol is unchanged (no field moved).
2. **When kept state starts and ends.** `PieceMoments` starts when the view builds, notes a change only when a snapshot
   brings one, clears on a new puzzle id, and a view built mid-round notes nothing (the first snapshot is suppressed) —
   `piece_moments_test`'s five cases. With no round there is no view, so nothing is held; there is no timer.
3. **How a new state combines with the others.** The motion is a pure function of the snapshots and the view's clock: it
   already stops with `effects: false` (the setting), and a pause is a snapshot whose pieces do not change, so nothing
   moves. There is no new pause/hide/away state; the painters draw the same states they drew before.
4. **Stay in the job's folder.** Every file written is in `packages/puzzle_game` or `docs/design/` (the mirror PNGs,
   regenerated by their own test), both in `allow:`. `git status` also lists `packages/puzzle_game/pubspec.yaml` and the
   root `pubspec.lock`, both in `allow:`; `tv_test_app/windows/flutter/generated_*` changed line endings when the checks
   ran `flutter pub get` in that app, not by hand.
5. **Stop rather than overstep.** Nothing needed a file outside `allow:`. One brief rule contradicts another (5 against
   9) and the choice is question 1; the tv failure is question 2.

## Questions

1. **Rule 5 against rule 9.** The re-measured `glyphEm` (0.55) is right for the text, but the camera uses it to choose the
   HUD's form, so eight pinned forms move (list above), including the rocket's race HUD at 852 × 393 falling back to the
   short form and the whole match's house HUD at 1024 × 768. That is a layout change by rule 9's wording, and rule 5's
   numbers can't be met without it: 0.5 is under "Them 3/6". I kept rule 5's numbers and pinned the new forms. If rule 9
   wins, the HUD needs a width estimate that is not one constant per character (per-string widths, or the form chosen on
   the real text), which is a camera change this job doesn't allow.
2. **`tv_test_app/test/home_look_test.dart` fails after 19:00.** Its five colour tests compare the app's style against a
   Sunny `tvLook`, but `pumpApp` is called without `clock:`, so the app takes `paletteFor(auto, DateTime.now())`, which is
   Night from 19:00 to 07:00. It passed in the day and failed when this job's checks ran at 19:38 and 19:48. Not caused by
   this job (the file and toy_look are unchanged). Fixing it is one `clock: () => DateTime(2026, 1, 1, 12)` per `pumpApp`
   call, as `tv_fit_test.dart` already does; it is outside this brief, so I left it.
