1. docs/streaming-marker.md: the spec both sides follow, short. One row of large high-contrast cells (black/white, each cell 16x16 px at a 1920x1080 picture, at the top-left corner with a 1-cell white-on-black quiet border; the decoder scales positions by picture width/1920). Content, in order: 2 sync cells (white, black), a 16-bit frame counter (wraps), a 32-bit host time (milliseconds of the SAME host clock the press probe uses for a probe press's host time, low 32 bits), one press-flip cell (flips colour on the frame in which a probe press was applied, holds until the next press), a 4-bit checksum (xor of nibbles). Most significant bit first. Dark = 0 below luminance 80, light = 1 above 160, after the decoder's 3x3-centre average per cell (state the thresholds as the rule that survives H.264 at 20 Mbit/s and a blur). You may refine sizes and bit counts if you say why in the spec; it must stay unambiguous and carry a short table of TEST VECTORS (value to cell string) that the Dart and the JS tests both use.
