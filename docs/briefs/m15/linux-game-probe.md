Background: the streaming server design puts the game (a Flutter desktop app) in a Linux container, drawn off-screen by the GPU, captured and encoded with NVENC. An earlier probe proved NVENC works in tp-stream and its containers, and that OpenGL there goes through Mesa's D3D12 layer (`GALLIUM_DRIVER=d3d12`, device `/dev/dxg`), with a trivial GL test at 68 frames a second at 1080p. Unknown: whether our real app builds for Linux, and whether it draws fast enough there.
