Owner (verbatim): "can we do a 'shuffle' toggle on the card? If 'shuffle' is enabled, next will pick another random puzzle in the category but not repeat previously played ones. It will end once all puzzles have been played. Ideally we should have flexibility but simplicity: 1. Allow player to pick a single puzzle they want to play 2. After that puzzle completes, they can go to the next puzzle in the list or shuffle in that category 3. If all puzzles are completed in that category, prompt user if they want to continue to the next difficulty and continue or go back to the selection screen."
Design (decided; outside a night only; a night keeps its single Next on the scoreboard):
1 A "series" starts when the player picks one specific puzzle on the picker; it remembers the played ids of that level (in memory, not saved). "Any <level> puzzle" is unchanged (it draws from job b009's bag).
2 The results card after a series round gets: Next puzzle, a Shuffle toggle (a switch with the label from host_text, remembered for the series, default off), Play again, Change teams, Back. Next with Shuffle off plays the next puzzle in the library's list order that is not yet played (wrapping past the end); with Shuffle on it plays a random not-yet-played puzzle of the level. Same teams as the round just played. The card uses a Wrap so it never overflows on a phone host.
3 When every puzzle of the level has been played in the series, Next puzzle is replaced by a prompt (a card or dialog with host_text strings) "You have played every <level> puzzle": Continue to the next level (starts the series on that level's first puzzle, or a random one if Shuffle is on, with a fresh played set) or Back to the selection screen. On the last level (no next level exists), only Back (and Play again) are offered. Hard does not exist yet: the level order comes from the Level enum, only levels with puzzles count.
4 Tests (each fails on the old code): Next after a hand-picked house plays the following puzzle and never one already played; with Shuffle on, N rounds play N different puzzles of the level and the (count+1)th shows the prompt; the prompt's Continue starts the next level's first puzzle with the same teams; the last level shows no Continue; a night's results still shows one Next; the toggle's state survives within the series; the results card draws on a phone-sized host without overflow.
