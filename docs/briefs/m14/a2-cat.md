The owner approved the mock-ups (Nov 9, 2026: "Mockups look good."). The cat follows G3 exactly; Claude's design for building it:
1. **`CatChaos`**, its own file(s) in `puzzle_game`, stepped by the match after each side's tick, from the match's seeded random (the host's) so a test can replay it. States: away → warning (2 s: the `meow` sound by name, a paw at the edge of that side, the cat's shadow on the exact target piece) → walk → bat → leave. Its clock stands still whenever the round does.
2. **Targeting and limits (G3)**: the side ahead in pieces placed (a draw: either); never the same side twice in a row; none in the first 20 s or the last 10 s; A little: at most once a round, only a loose or tray piece, batted to another free spot on that side (a spot where its tray box fits, as the tray rule checks); Party: at most twice, and once a round it may knock a placed piece loose, only on a side ahead by at least one piece, never the picture's last piece or one placed in the last 3 s: the piece goes back to that side's tray with its outline glowing, and the side keeps its points. On one board (solo, team) it visits that board and never knocks a placed piece. "Never in the night's last round": the night (job c1) sets Chaos off for that round, so the cat needs no night rule; say so in a comment.
3. **Shooing**: a worker on the cat pressing A during the warning or the walk shoos it; a shoo during the warning always works. It runs off and stays away for the rest of the round. +1 point to the shooer's side (a1's hook; × `pointsScale`, with a pop-up), and `catsShooed` +1 for the shooing player in `MatchResult.round`'s stats. A on the cat shoos rather than picks up.
4. **The Chaos setting**: `PuzzleSettings.chaos` (`off`, `little`, `party`; the library's default `off`, so every existing rules test passes unchanged, N12). The host saves it with the other puzzle settings, default **A little**, and Puzzle settings shows it as three buttons ("Off", "A little", "Party") with one line under it. `providers.dart` is at 394 lines: move the puzzle settings' provider and its saving into a file of its own first, by a pure move.
5. **Snapshot** (still version 2, unreleased): a top-level `cat` key (state, side, position, target piece, warning time left) only while the cat is out; a frame without it means no cat. docs/protocol.md lists it.
6. **Drawing** (the approved frame 3): the cat walks in from that side's edge, bats the piece, runs off; the paw and the shadow during the warning; a knocked piece's glowing outline in the tray. With effects off it appears and goes without the walk, and the cat still comes (G5). Nothing flashes.
7. **a1's follow-ups**: (Q1) the phone host's HUD panel gets a points line in a night: the camera reserves the line, `phoneHud` draws it; make room in `match_painter.dart` (400) and `camera.dart` (399) by moving a function out of each first; (Q2) each board in the snapshot carries its side (`sd`), and `MatchSnapshot.points` reads it instead of the board's order; (Q3) `pointsScale` and `night` are validated in `ranges` like `timeLimitS`.
