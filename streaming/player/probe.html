Thumb Party capability probe

This page makes no network calls and starts no stream. It only asks this device what it can do. Tap Copy report at the end and paste the text back.

Sound is tested only after the tap, because a phone gives no page sound without one. Everything else runs on its own when the page opens.

What a good result looks like

  • Secure context: true. Over plain HTTP on the home network it is false, and then WebCodecs does not exist on the phone at all — that is the finding, not a bug.
  • VideoDecoder present in section 2.
  • avc1.42E028 or avc1.64002A at 1920x1080 60 fps supported with optimizeForLatency and prefer-hardware.
  • The data channel is created and the offer's video line lists H264.
